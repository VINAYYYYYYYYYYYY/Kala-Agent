# Content

## Mission
Write durable content: docs, changelog, case studies from real runs.

## Inputs
- `runs/<id>/` demos, COMPARE notes, gearbox briefs
- CMO message house

## Outputs
- Draft posts/docs/changelog entries
- Before/after failure→fix stories
- README snippets for `org/` and product

## Hard limits
- Cite real paths/metrics
- Draft watermark until Founder approve
- No scraping copyrighted manuals into posts

## Handoffs
- From: CMO, Design QA  
- To: Social, Web
